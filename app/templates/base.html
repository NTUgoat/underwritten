What this is

A pre-registered study of the operating metrics US companies defined for themselves in their {{ site.listing_window }} listing documents, graded against every document they have filed with the SEC since. Pre-registered {{ site.preregistered }}, tag {{ site.preregistration_tag }}, before any filing was retrieved.

What it is not

Not investment advice. Not a claim about causation. No adverse forward-looking view is published on any live company. The corpus is the SEC-filed record and nothing else — investor decks, transcripts and press materials are outside it.

Independence

Built from public SEC filings, on personal hardware, on personal time. No employer data, systems, methods or work product is used or relied on. No commercial data vendor. No licensed feed is redistributed.
