How one measure is graded

A worked example, not a result. The candidate below has not been ruled on: what you can see is the evidence a human is given before deciding, which is the whole of what the machine is allowed to do.

    The promise

    A company writes its own measure into its listing document.

  2. 02

    The corpus

    Every document that company has filed or furnished since, not only its annual reports.

  3. 03

    Where it turned up

    The measure survives, but mostly outside the annual report.
