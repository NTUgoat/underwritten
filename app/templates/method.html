Exclusions

Two kinds. Cohort exclusions record candidates that failed an inclusion test — no company is ever removed for its result. Outcome exclusions are the two classes of filing that Amendment 1 removes from the primary adverse-event variable; both are counted and labelled rather than dropped.

Outcome exclusions, Amendment 1

labelruleapplied
SECTOR_WIDE_WARRANT_RESTATEMENT 8-K Item 4.02 filed on or after 2021-04-12 whose text cites the SEC staff statement on warrants issued by special purpose acquisition companies, or attributes the non-reliance to warrant or Class A share classification. Evidence about one SEC statement, not about an issuer. {{ exclusions_applied.get('SECTOR_WIDE_WARRANT_RESTATEMENT')|num }}
MECHANICAL_DESPAC_DELISTING Form 25 or 25-NSE filed within ±30 days of an 8-K carrying Item 2.01 — the predecessor shell's routine delisting at merger close. Genuine later delistings are unaffected. {{ exclusions_applied.get('MECHANICAL_DESPAC_DELISTING')|num }}

Both exclusions make the primary hypothesis harder to support, not easier, and the sensitivity re-runs the primary with the warrant restatements restored. The amendment, with the four filings it was confirmed against.
