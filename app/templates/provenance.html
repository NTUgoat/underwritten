Every document, and its hash

A SHA-256 is recorded for each retrieved document at the moment it is read, on the same code path as the fetch. Any reader can pull the same URL from sec.gov, hash it, and confirm it is byte-identical to the document this study read. A scheduled job re-fetches random cited spans and fails if a stored verbatim span no longer matches its source.

Corpus boundary

The corpus is every document an issuer filed or furnished to EDGAR: 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F, 40-F, 8-K and 6-K, including furnished earnings-release exhibits. Investor decks, earnings-call transcripts, websites and press materials not furnished to EDGAR are outside it.

Every claim this study makes is about the SEC-filed record and nothing more. A metric called discontinued here may still appear in an investor deck. Where the boundary is material to a specific company, the state is not determinable, not discontinued.

{# Asked often enough to be worth answering before it is asked: the corpus choice is a decision, not a default, and the reason is that nobody has to take this study's word for anything. #}

Why this corpus

EDGAR is the only filings corpus that lets a stranger audit this study for nothing: a complete bulk submissions archive, full-text search across every filer, a machine-readable items taxonomy that classifies corporate events without anyone hand-labelling them, and a published fair-access policy that states exactly how fast the same documents may be re-fetched.

That is the whole reason. The question this study asks is not a US question — whether management goes on reporting the yardstick it chose for itself is a question about any listing venue. But the evidence is US-only, because this is where the method can be checked rather than merely argued for, and a version of this study nobody could audit would not have been worth doing. It remains one cohort in one market, and METHOD.md §12.6 says so rather than implying otherwise.

Fair access

All access respects the SEC fair-access policy: a declared User-Agent carrying a real contact address, and a hard rate limit enforced in the client rather than left to the caller. The limit is set below the published ceiling deliberately.

No commercial data vendor is used. No licensed feed is redistributed. Where a price is needed it is derived from filed value divided by filed shares in Form 13F, taken as the median across at least three unrelated large filers, with cross-filer dispersion published. That is a licensing decision and is stated as one.

Independence

Built entirely from public SEC filings, on personal hardware, on personal time. No employer data, systems, methods or work product is used, referenced or relied on. Where the author has a personal position in a cohort company it is disclosed on the page where that company appears.

Dates

This publication began on {{ site.preregistered }}. The documents it reads go back to 2019. Those are two different claims and this site never conflates them: where a figure is described as historical it refers to the age of the underlying filed record, not to the age of this research.
