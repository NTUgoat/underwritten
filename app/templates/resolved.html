No case yet

A case enters this page only when its outcome is already a matter of public record, and it carries the filing that settles it. None of the fifty issuers has reached that bar since the corpus was read.

Why the page is empty rather than absent

The four qualifying outcomes are listed beside this, and each is a filing type rather than a judgement. An issuer joins this page when one of them appears in the filed record — not when the study forms a view about it. That order is the point: the adverse conclusion follows the document, never the other way round.

What qualifies a case

  • Restatement filed 8-K Item 4.02, non-reliance on previously issued financial statements — excluding the April 2021 SPAC warrant wave, which is evidence about one SEC staff statement and not about an issuer.
  • Delisting effected Form 25 or 25-NSE — excluding the mechanical predecessor delisting filed within thirty days of a de-SPAC completion 8-K.
  • Enforcement concluded A concluded action on the public docket, not an open investigation and not a press report of one.
  • Take-private completed Completed, not announced.
