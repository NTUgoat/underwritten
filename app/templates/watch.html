Still reading, every Monday

The fifty companies in this study go on filing whether or not anyone is watching them. A scheduled job re-reads every one of their EDGAR filing indexes each week, flags anything that counts as an adverse event under the pre-registered definition, and commits what it found. It runs without the author, and a week in which it finds nothing is published as a result rather than left blank.

{# ------------------------------------------------------- adverse events --- #}

Adverse events

The same four signals the study's confirmatory test uses, applied to filings that arrived after it: non-reliance on previously issued financials (8-K Item 4.02), an auditor change (Item 4.01), a late periodic filing (NT 10-K or NT 10-Q), and a delisting (Form 25 or 25-NSE). Amendment 1's two exclusions apply here as well, so neither the April 2021 SPAC warrant wave nor a mechanical de-SPAC delisting raises an alarm on this page.
